## 1. The headline finding: the tool is currently wrong on its single most load-bearing fact

The FCNR(B) subsidised-hedging window is the strongest BD trigger in the product. It appears in a live countdown KPI, the RBI risk card, the deadline calendar, the glossary, the service map, the playbook, and the cycle log — eleven places.

**The window closed on 31 August 2026.** The RBI announced on 15 August that it was closing the deposit-mobilisation window a month early, after the scheme pulled in $52.3bn of FCNR(B) deposits ($56.85bn across all three routes through 13 August). Banks had until 11 September to execute the related swaps. The ECB and OFCB routes continue to 31 December 2026.

The dashboard still says 30 September, and its countdown KPI today renders **"23d to FCNR window close"** for a window that shut a week ago. A partner who opened this in a client meeting this morning would have pitched a funding arbitrage that no longer exists.

This is precisely the failure mode `PARTNER-REVIEW.md` §1.2 named: *"One wrong date or stale price in a client meeting permanently ends the tool's credibility."* It is not a modelling problem — it is the absence of a regulatory watch loop, which is exactly what `regwatch-mcp` was scoped to solve. The event happened three weeks ago and nothing in the system noticed, because nothing in the system is *looking*.

**Correction to ship:** window closed 31 Aug 2026 (advanced from 30 Sep); swap execution ran to 11 Sep; ECB/OFCB routes remain open to 31 Dec 2026 — *which is still a live, dated advisory trigger, 115 days out.* The story survives; it moves from "hedging-cost arbitrage for banks" to "foreign-currency borrowing window for corporates."

Sources: [Business Standard, 17 Aug 2026](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/rbi-to-close-fcnr-b-forex-swap-facility-window-on-31-august-126081700102_1.html) · [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/business/banking/rbi-brings-forward-deadline-for-banks-to-raise-fcnr-deposits-after-strong-response-1.500641215) · [EcoNiti, 15 Aug 2026](https://www.econiti.org/daily-news/2026-08-15/2026-08-15-rbi-closes-fcnr-b-swap-window-early/). ## 2. The second finding: the validation clock was being reset every night

`scripts/pipeline.py` truncates score history to the last 26 entries on every run:

```python
history["dates"] = history["dates"][-26:]
```

The pipeline has run unattended and successfully every day from 17 July to 6 September — 52 consecutive days, a genuine achievement for a zero-dependency stdlib pipeline. But `data/history.json` today contains only **12 August → 6 September**. Four weeks of real, hard-won observations were silently discarded.

This matters more than it looks. `BRAINSTORM.md` §2.6 sets the validation gate at twelve weeks of history; the rolling truncation guarantees the tool can *never* reach it. It would have sat at "26 days of history" forever while appearing to accumulate.

**The data is recoverable.** Every daily commit carries the file, and 51 commits touch it going back to `55970a6` (17 July). Reconstructing the union of all committed snapshots restores the full 52-day series. Fix the cap (2 years), backfill from git, and the twelve-week validation gate arrives around 9 October 2026 rather than never.

## 3. What a Big 4 partner in this seat values — confirmed, with one revision

The five-point framing in `PARTNER-REVIEW.md` §1 holds up and I am not restating it. Current research confirms two things and revises one.

**Confirmed — the vocabulary is the billing surface.** Ind AS 109 hedge documentation, effectiveness-testing methodology and disclosure remain the live compliance surface for Indian corporates, and the 2026 Ind AS amendment rules tightened financial-instrument and hedge-accounting disclosure further ([CAclubindia](https://www.caclubindia.com/news/ind-as-amendment-rules-2026-new-changes-in-financial-instruments-hedge-accounting-disclosures-26735.asp), [Nexdigm](https://www.nexdigm.com/insights_post/ind-as-109-financial-instruments/)). The hedge-effectiveness calculator remains the single most sellable feature — it is a *standard*, so the answer is objectively right or wrong, and it is the test the client's own auditor will run.

**Confirmed — the CFO framing.** Commodity hedging has moved from a treasury housekeeping task to an explicit strategic bet with direct margin and pricing consequences ([Global Finance](https://gfmag.com/capital-raising-corporate-finance/commodity-hedging-dilemma/)). That is the register the ₹-crore conversion and CFaR outputs need to speak in — not percentages.

**Revised — "is it current?" is a harder bar than the tool assumed.** The July review treated currency as a weekly-cycle problem. The FCNR(B) miss shows it is a *daily regulatory watch* problem. Prices refresh nightly; **rules do not, and rules are what this tool sells.** A price that is a week stale is an annoyance. A rule that is three weeks stale is a wrong answer delivered with confidence. This moves `regwatch-mcp` from P3 nice-to-have to the highest-value component in the whole back-end plan.

## 4. Where the models still are not what they claim

The July removals were the right call and the honesty discipline holds — the forecast model correctly refuses to call itself a probability, and the removed families stayed removed. But three gaps remain between what the model cards say and what the code does.

| Model | State | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| `mQuant` | EWMA vol from real series where available ✅ | The 5,000-path draw is parametric normal with a 10%×2.4 jump — **not** historical simulation. No VaR/ES computed from actual returns, so nothing a treasury team can reconcile against their own numbers. |
| `mTail` | `const gpd=null` — EVT is **switched off** | Falls back to `ES99 × 1.35`, a scaling constant. The removal of the circular fit was correct; the replacement (a real POT fit on market returns) was never built. |
| `mTail` → `copulaES` | Correlations are real ✅ | **Volatilities are not.** `vols = 0.05 + rating/5 × 0.10` uses the 1–5 rating even though real EWMA vols for the same series sit in `CDATA.vols`. A real correlation matrix driven by fake volatilities produces a joint-stress number that is neither. |
| `RSCORE` | Velocity computed for 3 risks ✅ | Exposure and concentration hand-typed for all 8; 5 of 8 risks have a single distinct score across 26 observations — they cannot move, so they can never be validated. |
| `mNetwork` | Works | Card still says "34-company universe"; the universe is 87. Stale text in a governance disclosure. |

Also live: the EUA mark is a manual print from 16 July, now seven weeks stale, on a card that tells steel clients they carry a *hedgeable EUA exposure*; `cbam_ref.asof` is `null` and renders as "None" in the UI.

**None of these are architecture problems.** The skeleton is right. Every one is "replace a constant with a computation on data the pipeline already fetches."

## 6. Build plan

Architecture is unchanged from `PARTNER-REVIEW.md` §5 and still costs nothing to run. One addition: a shared `engine/` package in **pure standard-library Python**, imported by both the CI pipeline and the MCP servers, so the number an agent gets from a tool call is computed by *the same code* that produced the number on the page. No divergence is possible. The pipeline's zero-dependency property is preserved deliberately — a nightly job that cannot break on a dependency resolution is worth more than convenient array syntax.

```
engine/           pure-stdlib risk math — one implementation, three consumers
  stats.py        returns, EWMA, rolling vol, percentiles, correlation
  var.py          historical-simulation + parametric + MC VaR/ES; Kupiec & Christoffersen
  garch.py        GARCH(1,1) by MLE (Nelder–Mead, no SciPy)
  evt.py          POT/GPD fit, mean-excess diagnostics, tail quantiles & ES
  copula.py       Gaussian & t copula, tail dependence, df estimation
  hedge.py        Ind AS 109 dollar-offset + regression effectiveness
  cfar.py         Cash-Flow-at-Risk → ₹ crore EBITDA at risk

scripts/pipeline.py   fetch (unchanged) → engine → data/latest.json + data/risk.json
mcp/                  three FastMCP stdio servers over the same engine
  india_markets_mcp   spot, series, history, hedgeability lookups
  risk_engine_mcp     var, es, garch, gpd, copula, hedge_effectiveness, cfar
  regwatch_mcp        RBI / EC / PIB watch + diff_since  ← the FCNR(B) miss
```

**Phase A — correctness (ship first; it is wrong on screen right now).**
FCNR(B) dates across all eleven sites; history cap → 2 years plus git backfill; copula reads real vols; EUA/CBAM staleness surfaced with age; stale universe text.

**Phase B — real model calculations.**
Historical-simulation VaR/ES (1d/10d/1m) on 5y of real returns, cross-checked against parametric and MC; GARCH(1,1) by maximum likelihood; GPD peaks-over-threshold on real return tails with mean-excess diagnostics; t-copula with estimated degrees of freedom; CFaR per company in ₹ crore; Ind AS 109 hedge effectiveness. Published to `data/risk.json` and consumed by the page.

**Phase C — MCP servers.**
Three stdio servers over `engine/`, registered in `.mcp.json`, so the weekly cycle agent computes rather than asserts — every figure in a cycle becomes tool-output-backed.

**Deliberately still not built:** price forecasting, a credit module, multi-user auth. `BRAINSTORM.md` §Tier 4 explains why, and nothing in this review changes it.

## 7. What this does not fix

Backtest validation still needs calendar time. With the cap fixed and history backfilled the series runs from 17 July, so the twelve-week gate lands around **9 October 2026** — and only for the three market-driven risks. The five curated risks have never moved and cannot be validated until exposure and concentration are computed rather than typed. That work is real and is not in this build; the honest position until then is the one the Validation tab already takes.

Cost-base shares remain estimates from segment disclosure. They are labelled as estimates and should stay that way.
